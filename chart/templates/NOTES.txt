OpenChamber has been deployed!

Before accessing the service, ensure the secret exists:

  kubectl create secret generic {{ .Values.secretName }} \
    --from-literal=OPENCODE_GO_API_KEY=sk-... \
    --from-literal=GITHUB_PAT=ghp_... \
    --from-literal=OPENCHAMBER_UI_PASSWORD=your-password

Access the service:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }} 3000:3000

Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.
